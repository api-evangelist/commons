# Commons

> Commons is an Oakland, California consumer climate-fintech company (founded 2018 by Sanchali
> Seth Pal, known as Joro until March 2023). Its free iOS and Android app links bank accounts and
> credit cards via Plaid and scores every transaction with a proprietary carbon-estimation engine
> (the Carbonizer) into estimated kilograms of CO2e, alongside budgeting, savings goals, brand
> sustainability ratings, rewards and an optional paid carbon-offset membership.

## API surface

Commons publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-09 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no `/llms.txt`
and no `/.well-known/` discovery document on any Commons-controlled host. The product is a
direct-to-consumer mobile application; the only third-party API in the stack that Commons
documents publicly is **Plaid**, which it consumes (it does not resell or expose it).

Hosts probed: `www.thecommons.earth` (Webflow), `brands.thecommons.earth` (Next.js),
`exchange.thecommons.earth` (Softr), and the legacy Joro hosts `joro.tech` / `getjoro.com`.
`api.thecommons.earth`, `docs.thecommons.earth` and `developer.thecommons.earth` do not resolve.
